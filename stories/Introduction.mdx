# The Inkslap brand

<div style={{ height: '8px', background: 'linear-gradient(45deg, #80CCEF 0%, #F7D94D 50%, #FFA81B 100%)', borderRadius: '999px', margin: '24px 0 40px 0' }} />

This is the documented brand and design system for **Inkslap** — a B2B marketplace for custom corporate gifts, operated by NCI Trading Co., Ltd. and based in Taipei.

The contents were extracted directly from the live site at `https://inkslap.com` and cross-referenced with the company brand brief. Where the live site exhibits drift between near-identical values, this design system collapses them to a single canonical token. The drift catalogue lives under **Audit → Findings**.

---

## What Inkslap does

Inkslap is a one-stop platform for putting a logo on a product. Companies upload a logo, configure a customised gift (mugs, pens, apparel, notebooks, red envelopes), see an instant preview with AI-generated design options, get an instant quote, and place the order. The promise: a process that traditionally takes thirty to forty-five days, done in fifteen minutes.

**Trusted by:** OKX, Pokémon, Porsche, Sotheby's, Christie's, Netflix, Uber, Uber Eats, BlackRock, KFC, Volkswagen, Western Digital, Taipei 101, National Palace Museum, Herman Miller, Keith Haring, Shantell Martin, BenQ, CSD, Dentsu, Hongkuo, Max&Co, Namesake, TatlerAsia, Live.

## Voice in one line

Direct. Second-person. Time-quantified. The brand promises specific numbers (15 minutes, 7 days, 14 days) rather than vague claims, and uses the informal `you` everywhere — even in the About page.

**The tagline:** The most trusted corporate gifting platform.
**The promise:** A moment to complete selection, design, quoting, and ordering.
**The verb:** Upload your logo.

## How to read this site

### For designers

Start with **Foundations**. Colour, typography, spacing, and shadows are documented as tokens with exact values. The signature brand gradient and the brand-tinted shadow are signature treatments — note them.

### For developers

Every value here lives as a CSS custom property in [src/styles/global.css](https://github.com/RiaanMOHA/inkslap-storybook/blob/main/src/styles/global.css). Reference by token name; never use raw hex or px values in implementation.

```css
.cta-primary {
  background: var(--color-brand-blue);
  border-radius: var(--radius-md);
  padding: var(--space-button-md);
  box-shadow: var(--shadow-brand);
}
```

### For writers

**Guidelines → Voice & Tone** and **Writing Style** are canonical. Sentence case is the default everywhere. Italics are not used. The brand name is `Inkslap` in body copy and `inkslap` only in the wordmark.

### For QA and audit

**Audit → Findings** lists everything that drifted on the live site and what the documented canonical value is. Fixing the site to match this design system is the path to consistency.

---

## English-first

The current live site is Traditional Chinese-only with a stub `/en` route. The documented brand inverts that: English is the canonical language, with Traditional Chinese alongside as the translation reference. Every page in this Storybook shows the English copy first; Chinese appears below it where relevant.

The bilingual handling pattern is documented under **Guidelines → Bilingual**.
