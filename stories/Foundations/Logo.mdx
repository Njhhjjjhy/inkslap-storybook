# Logo

The Inkslap logo is a lowercase wordmark `inkslap` set tight, with a single 4-point orange sparkle floating above and slightly left of the dot on the `i`. The sparkle is the only ornament. There is no icon mark or monogram.

## Clear space

The live site does not enforce explicit clear space. The recommended minimum is **the height of the wordmark itself** on all four sides. In practice on the live site, the header leaves ~16 px from the page edge and ~70 px before the nav begins.

## Casing rule

The brand name has two forms:

- **`inkslap`** — lowercase, in the wordmark only. The wordmark is a closed asset; do not retypeset it as body copy.
- **`Inkslap`** — title case, used in every other written context. This is the canonical body form.

The live site exhibits drift on this — `INKSLAP` appears as image `alt` text and `InkSlap` (camel case) appears once in the KeyFeatures copy. Both are incorrect. See **Audit → Findings** for the full list.
