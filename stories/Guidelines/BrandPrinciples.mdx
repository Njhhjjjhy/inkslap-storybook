# Brand Principles

What Inkslap stands for, and how those values show up in every product decision.

---

## Mission

> The most trusted corporate gifting platform.
>
> `最受信賴的企業禮贈品平台`

A B2B marketplace that compresses a thirty-to-forty-five-day procurement process into fifteen minutes. Companies upload a logo, see an instant preview, get a quote, and place the order — without phone calls, spreadsheets, or design back-and-forth.

---

## The North Star metaphor

The About page positions the brand explicitly: **we are your North Star in the procurement maze.** The metaphor is deliberate — procurement feels lost without guidance, and Inkslap orients buyers toward the right decision without them having to learn the trade.

Use the metaphor sparingly in marketing copy. It should not appear inside the product UI itself.

## Problems we solve

The brand frames procurement's pain in three explicit pieces:

| Pain | What we solve |
|------|---------------|
| **Time cost is too high** | Traditional flow: 30–45 days of phone calls. Our flow: 15 minutes online. |
| **Experience is inconsistent** | Standardised sampling, colour management, supplier vetting. Same outcome every order. |
| **Supply chain is fragmented** | One platform connects buyers, brands, factories, and customisation shops. |

---

## How we work

Three methods that turn the principles above into operations:

1. **Compress the flow** — One upload, an instant preview, an instant quote. No back-and-forth.
2. **Standardise the sample** — Every customisation method (UV, screen, transfer, embroidery, laser, DTF) follows a documented sampling SOP.
3. **Visualise everything** — Dashboards for delivery dates, batch status, and supplier output. Buyers and sellers see the same data.

---

## What we are not

The brand has a clear negative space. Inkslap is **not**:

- A general-purpose print-on-demand platform. We work on bulk corporate orders, not single-item personal customisation.
- A marketing agency. We do not write campaigns or design brand assets — we apply existing brand assets to physical goods.
- A design tool. The AI-generated previews exist to confirm the upload looks right, not to invent brand identities.

If a request falls outside these bounds, route the conversation elsewhere rather than stretching the brand to fit.

---

## Decision framework

When in doubt, ask:

1. **Does it shorten the path to a finished gift?** If yes, ship it. If no, drop it.
2. **Does it raise quality without raising friction?** Speed without quality is not the goal; both at once is.
3. **Does it work for an occasional buyer?** PAs and office managers — not procurement specialists — are the primary user. Specialist-only tooling is wrong.
4. **Does it hold across both languages?** Every feature must work in English and Traditional Chinese from day one. See **Guidelines → Bilingual**.
